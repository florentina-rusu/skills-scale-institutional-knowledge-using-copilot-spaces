# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers research user needs and shape accessible, usable interactions and visual designs. They partner with the Product Manager on user outcomes and with Developers on feasible implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and identify usability needs
- Create and validate user flows, wireframes, and interface designs
- Include accessibility and usability considerations in design decisions
- Explain design intent and constraints to Developers and QA/Testing
- Incorporate feedback from users, stakeholders, and usability reviews

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, understandable, and accessible
- Validate design assumptions before they create costly rework
- Maintain alignment between user needs and shipped behavior

### Typical Communication
- Design artifacts and research summaries
- Design reviews with the Product Manager and Developers
- Usability and accessibility acceptance notes with QA/Testing

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
The Product Manager remains accountable for product priorities and outcomes. UX/UI Designers advise on user needs and experience; Developers advise on implementation feasibility. They work with QA/Testing to make usability and accessibility expectations verifiable.

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical design and help teams manage architecture, integration, and technical risks. They support Developers without replacing shared engineering ownership.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical designs, integration approaches, and engineering standards
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support Developers with design decisions and technical reviews
- Communicate technical options and trade-offs to the Product Manager and Project Manager
- Contribute to estimates and plans for technically complex work

### Goals
- Deliver secure, reliable, maintainable solutions
- Make technical decisions and trade-offs visible
- Reduce integration surprises and avoidable technical risk

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture decisions
- Design and code reviews with Developers
- Risk, dependency, and estimate updates with the Product Manager and Project Manager

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Technical Leads guide technical direction in collaboration with Developers, who remain responsible for implementation. They explain technical trade-offs to the Product Manager, who owns product prioritization, and to the Project Manager, who coordinates plans, risks, and dependencies.

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business processes, needs, and requirements so that proposed work can be understood, prioritized, and verified.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document workflows, requirements, and edge cases
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and impacts across business processes
- Help translate needs into clear backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Review requirements with stakeholders and delivery team members
- Support validation that delivered work meets agreed requirements

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and rework caused by unclear requirements
- Make business needs and process impacts visible
- Help ensure requirements can be tested and traced to outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements, workflow diagrams, and decision notes
- Backlog refinement with the Product Manager and Developers
- Acceptance-criteria reviews with QA/Testing and stakeholders

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
The Business Analyst gathers and clarifies needs with stakeholders and helps the Product Manager shape requirements; the Product Manager retains prioritization and product decisions. Business Analysts work with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify expected behavior, while the Project Manager coordinates dependencies and delivery planning.

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Coaches facilitate team practices and help teams improve their delivery flow. This role applies when it fits the team's working model.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agreed team ceremonies and collaborative planning
- Help surface blockers and support the team in resolving them
- Encourage continuous improvement and healthy team practices
- Help make workflow issues and delivery impediments visible
- Support the team in using its agreed delivery process

### Goals
- Improve collaboration and sustainable delivery
- Resolve or escalate impediments promptly
- Help teams learn and improve their working practices

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and improvement discussions
- Blocker and workflow updates with the team and Project Manager
- Retrospective actions and follow-up

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
The Scrum Master or Delivery Coach facilitates the team's process but does not take over the Project Manager's responsibility for project coordination, schedules, risks, or stakeholder communication. The Product Manager remains accountable for product priorities; the team remains responsible for delivery commitments and implementation decisions.

---

## DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps or Site Reliability Engineers help teams build, deploy, and operate services reliably. Their involvement may vary with the product and operational model.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on CI/CD pipelines, environments, deployment, and infrastructure
- Help define observability, reliability, and operational readiness needs
- Identify operational risks and contribute mitigations
- Support incident response and service recovery practices as appropriate
- Coordinate operational requirements for releases

### Goals
- Make releases safer and more repeatable
- Improve service reliability and observability
- Ensure operational risks are addressed before and after release

### Typical Communication
- CI/CD, environment, and operational-readiness documentation
- Reliability and incident updates with Developers and QA/Testing
- Release risks and dependencies with the Project Manager

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
DevOps/Site Reliability Engineers advise on operating and releasing services and work with Developers and QA/Testing to integrate operational checks into delivery. The Project Manager coordinates release plans and risks; the Product Manager makes product priority decisions.

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Leads help teams identify security and privacy requirements, assess risks, and ensure concerns are addressed through the appropriate review and escalation processes.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and risks
- Advise on controls, secure design, privacy practices, and reviews
- Help ensure findings are documented, assigned, and tracked to resolution
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Follow established security incident response and escalation procedures

### Goals
- Identify and address security and privacy risks early
- Make security and privacy responsibilities clear throughout delivery
- Support safe releases and appropriate incident response

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy requirements, reviews, and risk records
- Findings and mitigation tracking with Developers and QA/Testing
- Escalations through the documented security incident path

### Collaboration and Decision Boundaries
Security/Privacy Leads provide specialist guidance and raise risks through the established escalation path. Developers implement agreed controls, QA/Testing supports validation, and the Project Manager tracks delivery risks and dependencies. Product decisions remain with the Product Manager, subject to required security, privacy, and organizational controls.

---

## Applying These Roles

Role titles describe responsibilities, not mandatory headcount. Depending on project size and context, one person may cover multiple roles, responsibilities may be shared, or a specialist may be consulted only when needed. Teams should agree on role ownership, decision boundaries, and escalation paths during initiation or planning so responsibilities complement the existing Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders.
